Flight Dynamics Lab

Could a proposed flight match the satellite record and keep flying with its available fuel? The model follows its motion through the atmosphere, accounts for fuel use, and compares the resulting signals with the recorded transmissions. Here, each saved time has a readout of where the aircraft is and what it is doing.

Viewing the 12 November catalogue. The opening flight is one saved example from that snapshot. The 108-arrival cruise-level study and later descent results are separate. For the present geographical assessment, see Ryan’s current working area.

Which later results are outside this viewer?

The snapshot predates the lower-speed run of 12 November. That run (target Mach 0.650 to 0.630, its own speed rule) extended the compatible computed states north to 29.88°S, 98.35°E; its results are in the experiment record and are not shown here.
