The flight histories connected 18:22:12 → terminal dossier verified

Six complete flight histories, integrated continuously from the last primary-radar fix to the altitude where the weather record ends. Every number on this page comes from the retained records of the terminal campaign; nothing is recomputed here. The complete dataset, with an exporter that reproduces any registered flight as one chronological table, is identified at the foot of the page.

Conditional calculation. Intact airframe assumed; zero thrust after fuel exhaustion; pinned drag surrogate beyond its declared Mach 0.89 criterion (no aerodynamic validation above it); prescribed roll-out hypothesis; saved lift coefficient held; control law held unchanged after APU fuel exhaustion (flagged on every affected path); strict ERA5 weather with no near-surface extrapolation; no structural criterion. A model-support stop is not stall or breakup; APU exhaustion is not impact.

What happens after the final transmissions

From the saved states at 00:19:37, each response is carried forward under its unchanged control law. The high-speed descent peaks near Mach 0.89; of the 424 registered arms, 332 stop at the model-support boundary there (the surrogate's lift-limit fit meets the commanded lift coefficient — a model-domain marker, not an aircraft event), and 68 glide on. In those 68, the pitch oscillation damps over about four cycles into a quasi-steady glide; sampled Mach never again approaches the limit (peaks 0.814–0.828). APU fuel is exhausted at 00:24:21–00:24:26 in all 68. Every gliding flight leaves the lowest level of the weather record — 187–200 m above the sea — between 00:25:41 and 00:26:46 UTC, descending at 8.9–44 m/s at ground speed 179–209 m/s.

That last computed state is the terminal reporting endpoint. A separately labelled constant-velocity projection carries each flight the remaining 0.96–5.1 km to the sea surface (contact 00:25:47–00:26:53 UTC); it is a kinematic suffix, never a computed contact, and two alternative projections land within 0.3–3.9 km of it.

Export a saved flight

The complete dataset is a 249 MB archive, kept outside this repository and identified by checksum:

MH370_Flight_Histories_Data.zip · 249,256,676 bytes · SHA-256 3d3e5c3e0a4768c29abbed5baa3720cdf58fd0317a2420c068558a69eb8fe0ed

Independently audited 10 Nov 2026: all 274 manifest entries and 101 table inventories verify; both prescribed sample exports reproduce exactly; 1,624/1,624 internal joins have identical times and positions (98 powered-to-onset comparisons bitwise on the compared fields; 112 joins differ only at the last floating-point bit in velocity or lift-vector components). Audit record: reports/diagnostic/FLIGHT_HISTORIES_ARCHIVE_AUDIT in the data repository. A public download link will be added when the hosting location is established.

The included reader joins a parent history to its terminal branch and writes one chronological record — position, height, Mach, speeds, track, fuel, mass, atmosphere, satellite comparisons — with every original column and its source labelled:

python tools/export_flight.py --root . list
python tools/export_flight.py --root . export \
  --branch "TB|PRODUCTION|U_T0720_M0.75_C190_TM0.67_J1|+60|108|ROLLOUT_10S|PINNED_RISE|NOMINAL" \
  --out exports/C190_J1_right_108_roll10_pinned.csv.gz

That example reproduces 5,605 rows and 117 columns from 18:22:12.000000 to 00:25:43.160210 UTC. The exporter reproduces the retained records; it does not rerun the aircraft, atmosphere or satellite calculations. Where a calculation can also be rerun from pinned code and inputs, the repository's run directories say so, with expected results.
