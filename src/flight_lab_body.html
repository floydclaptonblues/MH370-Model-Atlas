What this run leaves open

This cruise run did not test the final transmissions, the descent or an electrical restart. A separate experiment has since computed terminal responses for the flights whose engine fuel runs out before 00:19:29; they are on the terminal responses page and are not displayed in this viewer. The timing recorded at 22:41, which this run did not use, has since been compared with each of its flights; that result is on the 22:41 page and is not displayed here either. No impact position has been computed. Intermediate levels, later climbs and changes of cruise speed were not part of this run. The frequency residuals at the three fitted times show no conflict with the adopted band under the frozen calibration; that is a diagnostic, partly inherited from the earlier frequency selection, rather than an independent confirmation.

The 22:41 measurement was not evaluated in this run. Prior access to that measurement is described in the method and disclosure record. The separate satellite audit found that admission of the earlier histories is sensitive to orbit and calibration choices.

Source records, downloads and numerical-check scope

The browser reads saved results only. It performs no flight integration, refitting, interpolation or fuel prediction. The source tables preserve all returned records; the explorer counts identical commands once per configuration and links their direction records.

One returned flight was repeated per eligible direction, for 218 checks. Those checks apply to the repeated flights; they do not validate every trial or repeat the searches that returned no accepted flight. Range-end records remain explicitly marked. Numerical repeatability is not physical positional accuracy.
