Four published statements said that 22:41 was excluded or not evaluated. Each was true of the experiment it described and is kept in that sense, with the later check named beside it:

- overview, terminal-responses section: "was excluded" now reads "was excluded from this experiment", with the later result;
- overview, "Where this leaves the published histories": the sentence that the comparison would be made after the population and predictions were fixed now says that this was done for the 266 flights, and no longer uses the word "blind";
- terminal-responses page, the "Measurement at 22:41" row and the corresponding limit;
- methods page, the two "22:41 was not evaluated" statements and the standing rule about the measurement.

The dated notes of 10 and 11 November and the archived methods material were not changed.

## Wording

The 51 flights outside the band are described as outside the declared criterion and as remaining possible, not as negated: they are outside by 0.03 to 4.20 µs, and neither the measurement nor the model is accurate at that scale. The 215 are described as meeting the criterion, not as confirmed. The check is called a withheld-measurement consistency check with disclosed prior exposure throughout.
