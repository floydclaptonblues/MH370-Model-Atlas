## Correction, later the same evening

Codex's audit of the same workbook found that two entries of the project's event table, the voice calls at 18:39 and 23:14, are rounded means of 51 and 29 received units and not single units. The section published an hour earlier said that all 19 entries were in the log "with the same timing and frequency". That sentence was wrong for those two entries and has been replaced: 17 single units are equal, and the two means are reproduced, with the points on which the table's description of them is incomplete. The data-repository cross-check was corrected first (commit `5eea308`), and the Atlas copies are pinned there. No count, table or figure of the 22:41 result changed.

The distances given for the excess beyond the band (about 5 m to 0.63 km) now say explicitly that they are measured along the line between aircraft and satellite and are not errors of position over the ground.

Codex's review also prepared a patch to the explanatory prose of the first publication. Its packet had not reached the publishing session when this correction was made, so that patch was not applied in that commit.

## Codex's ten substitutions on the 22:41 page

The packet arrived a few minutes later. Its patch was written against the first publication (`2de57d2`), and the page had advanced by three commits, so the patch was not applied as a patch. The ten documented text substitutions were carried onto the current `src/check2241_body.html` one by one. Nine matched their old text exactly. The seventh, the note that being outside the band does not mean shown to be impossible, had since gained one sentence about the ground-station log; Codex's replacement was applied and that sentence kept after it.

What the substitutions change: the measurement is said to have been outside the catalogue runner's fitting objective and selection criteria, where the page had said it was not used to build the flights; a model state is called a conditional reconstruction that does not establish the aircraft's position; the timing is related to the two-way path and the distances are called slant range, not displacement on the Earth's surface; statements that a prediction existed "before any prediction for 22:41" now say "for these catalogue flights"; the statement that neither measurement nor model is accurate to a few microseconds, and that two flights are not distinguished "in any physical sense", is replaced by what the check does not do, which is to carry measurement and model uncertainty into a statement about individual flights; the result for the terminal-study flights is described as an added consistency result that changes no stored terminal state or classification; and the prediction spread is no longer said to imply that the check could only separate some flights.

Checked after the substitutions: the placeholders of the page and how often each occurs are unchanged, the typed numbers are unchanged and in the same order, all table blocks are identical, and the compiled values are untouched.

One sentence outside Codex's ten was aligned with them: the overview summary said that "neither the measurement nor the model is accurate at that scale". It now gives the log's reporting step and burst scatter, which are measured, and says the check does not quantify how plausible a flight on either side of the edge is. Codex has not reviewed that sentence, nor the section on the ground-station log.

The record of the audit, the patch and the ten substitutions is in the data repository at `reports/diagnostic/CHECK_2241_CRUISE_266/inmarsat_source_audit_20261118/` (commit `bbc3402`). The private workbook and Codex's packet are in neither repository.

## Wording

The 51 flights outside the band are described as outside the declared criterion and as remaining possible, not as negated: they are outside by 0.03 to 4.20 µs, and neither the measurement nor the model is accurate at that scale. The 215 are described as meeting the criterion, not as confirmed. The check is called a withheld-measurement consistency check with disclosed prior exposure throughout.
