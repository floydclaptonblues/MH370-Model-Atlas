## Correction of the log interpretation, after Codex's follow-up review

Codex reviewed the publication at `85ec379` and the data repository at `bbc3402`. It accepted the ten substitutions and reproduced the arithmetic of the log section (the reporting grid, five dispersion calculations and all eleven rows of the offset table). It did not accept the interpretation placed around those numbers. That interpretation was written in the publishing session and was wrong in these respects:

- The 51 flights outside the band were said to lie inside the 20 µs reporting step and far inside the scatter of one burst, and so to remain possible. The comparison used the excess beyond the 43 µs boundary (0.03 to 4.20 µs) and stated the conclusion as if it were about the whole residual. The whole residuals are 43.03 to 47.20 µs in magnitude, larger than the step and larger than the 29 µs dispersion.
- A reported value was said to stand for a range of true values 20 µs wide. The step is the resolution of a reported estimate that also contains measurement error. The error statistic behind the ±43 µs criterion already includes that quantisation, so the step is no further allowance.
- The split of 215 and 51 was said not to be resolved by the measurement. Under the declared rule it is resolved. The offset table shows that the labels are sensitive to small hypothetical offsets. Its rows are not other readings the log could have given, and they are not probabilities.
- The 29 to 40 µs figures were presented as the standard deviation of a single burst, with the band expressed in standard deviations. They are descriptive dispersions of selected records that mix channels, receiving units and message types, use overlapping pairs, and in one case come from the whole log. The 22:41 measurement is on a different channel and receiving unit. They are not a calibrated error distribution for a measurement in flight.
- The 0.094 s between the model's time and the log's was said to matter for one flight and not for the others. The 0.05 to 0.06 µs comes from average slopes between exchanges an hour apart, not from the rate of change of any flight's prediction. How many labels would change at the logged time is not determined.

## Wording

The 51 flights outside the band are described as outside the declared criterion. The check alone does not establish that their histories are physically impossible, and they are not described as negated. They are outside by 0.03 to 4.20 µs; their whole residuals are 43.03 to 47.20 µs in magnitude. The 215 are described as meeting the criterion, not as confirmed. The fixed labels do not quantify how plausible a history near the boundary is. The check is called a withheld-measurement consistency check with disclosed prior exposure throughout.

An earlier version of this paragraph said the 51 "remain possible" because "neither the measurement nor the model is accurate at that scale". That reasoning is withdrawn; see the correction above.
